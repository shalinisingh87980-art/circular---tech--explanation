Waste sorting guide

🟢 Wet/organic waste — food scraps and vegetable peels.

🔵 Dry recyclable waste — clean paper, plastic, glass and metal.

🔴 Special waste — batteries, electronics, chemicals and hazardous items. Keep separate and use authorized collection services.

Follow your local waste collection rules. Not every plastic or paper item is recyclable.

How can we help?

  • Segregate waste at the source.
  • Reuse and repair products before replacing them.
  • Take batteries and electronics to authorized recyclers.
  • Share safe surplus food to prevent avoidable waste.
  • Reduce single-use products and unnecessary packaging.

Statistics are demo activity counts. They do not represent verified kilograms recycled, money saved or emissions reduced.
